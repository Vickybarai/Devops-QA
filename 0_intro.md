## 📋 What's Inside

| Version | Duration | Best For |
|---------|----------|----------|
| Standard | 60–90 sec | Main introduction round |
| Short | 30–40 sec | Quick intro / follow-up |
| Ultra-Short | 15–20 sec | Elevator pitch / brief mention |

---

## 🟢 Standard Version — 60 to 90 seconds

### Structure
1. **Introduction** — Who you are
2. **Education** — Degree & college
3. **Internship / Experience** — What you did
4. **DevOps Interest Trigger** — How you got into Cloud/DevOps
5. **Skills & Tools** — What you learned
6. **Current Focus** — What you're doing right now
7. **Career Objective** — What role you're looking for

### Script

> "My name is **[Your Name]**, and I'm currently a final-year **[Degree Name]** student from **[College Name]**.
>
> During my academic journey, I developed an interest in software development and cloud technologies. I also completed a **[duration]** internship in **[field]**, where I worked with a team on real-world projects and gained practical exposure to how applications are built and delivered in a professional environment.
>
> During that time, I also got the opportunity to deploy an application on AWS, which introduced me to how servers, cloud infrastructure, and deployment pipelines work. That experience sparked my interest in Cloud and DevOps practices.
>
> To strengthen my knowledge, I joined **[training institute / self-learning path]**, where I learned Linux fundamentals, AWS core services such as EC2, VPC, and IAM, along with tools like Git and Docker.
>
> Currently, I'm focusing on hands-on practice, improving my understanding of cloud infrastructure, automation, and deployment workflows.
>
> I'm now looking for a beginner-level Cloud or DevOps role, where I can contribute, learn from experienced engineers, and gradually grow into a strong DevOps professional."

## ✏️ How to Use

1. **Copy** the version that fits your situation
2. **Replace** all `[bracketed]` placeholders with your real details
3. **Read it out loud** 5–10 times until it sounds natural
4. **Time yourself** — stay within the suggested duration
5. **Tweak** words to match your speaking style (don't memorize word-for-word)

---

## 💡 Tips

- **Don't rush** — speak at a calm, steady pace
- **Be honest** — only mention tools you actually know
- **Make eye contact** — look at the interviewer, not the ceiling
- **Smile** — it sets a positive tone before you even start
- **Practice with a friend** — get feedback on clarity and confidence
