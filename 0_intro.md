 Introduction

🟢 1. Standard Version — 60–90 Seconds

Best for the main interview introduction round.

Good morning, sir.

My name is , and I am from Nagpur, Maharashtra. I have completed my Bachelor of Computer Applications and Master of Computer Applications.

I have a strong interest in Cloud Computing and DevOps, particularly in automation, CI/CD pipelines, and cloud infrastructure management.

I have worked on practical DevOps projects, mainly focusing on CI/CD automation using Jenkins and GitHub Actions. I have also worked with AWS services such as EC2, VPC, IAM, S3, RDS, and CloudWatch for infrastructure setup, networking, access management, and monitoring.

Apart from that, I have hands-on experience with Docker and Kubernetes for containerization and orchestration, and Terraform for Infrastructure as Code. I also have working knowledge of Linux, Git, Shell scripting, Maven, Nginx, and Tomcat.

My main focus is on automating deployment workflows, troubleshooting technical issues, and understanding how applications are deployed and managed in cloud environments.

I am currently looking for an entry-level DevOps Engineer opportunity where I can apply my practical knowledge, contribute to real-world projects, and grow as a DevOps professional.

Thank you.
---

🟡 2. Short Version — 30–40 Seconds

Best when the interviewer asks for a brief introduction.

Good morning, sir.

My name is Vicky Barai, and I am from Nagpur, Maharashtra. I have completed my BCA and MCA, and I am focused on building my career in DevOps and Cloud Computing.

I have worked on practical projects involving CI/CD pipelines using Jenkins and GitHub Actions, AWS cloud services, Docker, Kubernetes, and Terraform. I also have working knowledge of Linux, Git, and Shell scripting.

I am looking for an entry-level DevOps Engineer role where I can apply my technical skills and contribute to the team.

Thank you.
---

🔴 3. Ultra-Short Version — 15–20 Seconds

Best for quick introductions or networking.

Hi, my name is Vicky Barai, and I am from Nagpur. I have completed my MCA and am focused on DevOps and Cloud Computing, with practical experience in CI/CD, AWS, Docker, Kubernetes, and Terraform. I am looking for an entry-level DevOps Engineer opportunity to start my professional career.
---

How to Practise These Introductions

Version	Your objective	What to emphasize

Standard	Explain your profile and technical background	CI/CD, AWS, Docker, Kubernetes
Short	Communicate your profile quickly	Core skills and target role
Ultra-short	Create a clear first impression	Education, domain, and career objective.
